## 3. When a refund may be due

We investigate requests involving duplicate or incorrect charges, an unauthorized
transaction, failure to provide the purchased service, a verified technical failure
attributable to the responsible provider, or another case required by applicable law.
The appropriate remedy may be correction, re-performance where agreed, a partial
refund or a full refund, according to the facts and your legal rights. Reporting an
unauthorized charge may also require contacting your payment provider promptly.

A refund is not automatically excluded merely because an interaction was submitted
or a feature was opened. We consider whether the purchased service was actually
provided and whether the charge and relevant disclosures were correct.
