## Policy information

Published: 24 October 2026. Effective: 7 November 2026.

Substantive changes take effect after 14 days’ notice. The previous version is available below.

HyperChat is a product of STREAMHEART PRIVATE LIMITED. For refund or cancellation requests, contact ankit@hyperchat.space.

## 1. Scope and payment roles

This policy applies to HyperChat digital services supplied by Streamheart. Payments use a payment aggregator/gateway, and Streamheart settles creator funds. We keep transaction IDs, amounts and payout records, but not payer details as payment records. Provider processing does not eliminate our responsibilities for our service.

The purchase flow must identify the supplier, requested service, total price and any HyperChat fee, creator amount, processing fee or tax, with cancellation/refund conditions before confirmation. The applicable payment provider is identified there. This policy does not declare all charges non-refundable merely because they are digital. Mandatory Indian consumer and other statutory rights take precedence.

## 2. How to request help

Email ankit@hyperchat.space with your account/contact email, creator or service concerned, approximate date/time, transaction reference if available, what happened and the remedy requested. Screenshots or error text may help. Do not send passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details, bank credentials or private OBS source URLs.

We verify requests proportionately using necessary information. Our service target is acknowledgement within 48 hours and a reasoned resolution within 30 days, subject to shorter legal requirements. Request data follows the Privacy Policy; a refund request does not justify unnecessary identity-document collection.

## 3. Refund eligibility

We investigate duplicate or incorrect charges, unauthorized transactions, verified technical failures, undelivered purchased services and other cases required by law. An appropriate remedy may be correction, agreed re-performance, a partial refund or a full refund according to the facts and applicable rights. Report unauthorized charges to the payment provider promptly as well.

Submitting an interaction, opening a feature or beginning a session does not by itself prove full delivery or defeat a valid refund claim. Where a purchased interaction is rejected, expires or cannot be delivered, we assess whether the promised service was supplied and refund any amount legally due. Service fees cannot be retained for an unsupplied service merely by labeling them non-refundable. The actual charged-service and moderation rules must be disclosed before payment.

## 4. Completed interactions and creator responses

A correctly described and fully delivered digital service does not ordinarily create a refund entitlement solely because a viewer changes their mind or dislikes a creator's response, subject to mandatory rights. We do not guarantee audience engagement, attention or creator endorsement. These limitations do not excuse misrepresentation, deficient service or unlawful charges.

Queue delays and moderation can affect appearance time. If there is a material delivery failure, contact us for investigation rather than assume submission equals delivery.

## 5. Cancellation

Contact support promptly to cancel an interaction that has not yet been delivered. We check whether delivery can still be stopped and what remedy is due. Already broadcast content may not be retrievable, but valid refund and privacy rights remain.

If a recurring service is offered, its renewal amount, interval, cancellation method and effective cancellation timing must be disclosed before purchase. Cancellation stops future renewal according to those terms and applicable law; treatment of the current period depends on what was supplied and the applicable rights. No recurring charge is authorized by this policy alone.

Account closure, paid-service cancellation and withdrawal of data consent must be handled distinctly. We do not treat consent withdrawal as surrender of a lawful refund or permission for an unwanted future charge.

## 6. Approved refunds

We communicate the approved amount and relevant payment channel. Refunds are made to the original payment method where feasible and required. Our processing estimate is 7–14 business days after approval, subject to applicable mandatory deadlines. Payment-provider and bank crediting can affect when the refund appears; we give available references and investigate delays rather than guarantee an instant credit. If the provider supplies a different timeframe for a specific refund, we communicate it and comply with applicable mandatory deadlines.

Routine interaction/media access and required archival retention are different. The Privacy Policy explains the applicable processing-data/log minimum, financial record obligations, restricted access and erasure. A refund or account deletion request does not override legally required preservation, nor permit unrelated reuse.

## 7. Creator adjustments and complaints

Refunds, chargebacks and documented lawful holds may require settlement adjustments under the creator agreement. Creator Terms section 6.1 also sets out the platform rule allowing HyperChat to withhold a calendar month’s payout when a creator earns through HyperChat and begins using another monetization platform in that same month without advance notice. Questions or review requests about a hold can be sent to ankit@hyperchat.space. A creator payout hold does not remove a viewer’s applicable refund rights or either party’s mandatory legal remedies.

Requests may be refused for a lawful, evidenced reason, with an explanation and review route. Repeated requests alone do not prove fraud. Our internal decision is not unchallengeably final: payment-provider disputes, consumer remedies, competent courts and regulators remain available under applicable law. Contact ankit@hyperchat.space to request review or raise a grievance.
