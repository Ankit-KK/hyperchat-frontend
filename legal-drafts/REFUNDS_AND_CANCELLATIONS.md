# HyperChat Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Revised review draft, 24 October 2026 — not yet effective.** Adopt an effective
date only after completing the publication checks in README.md.

HyperChat is a product of **STREAMHEART PRIVATE LIMITED**. For refund or cancellation
requests, contact **ankit@hyperchat.space**.

## 1. Scope and payment roles

This policy applies to HyperChat digital services supplied by Streamheart. Payments
use a payment aggregator/gateway, and **Streamheart settles creator funds**. We keep
transaction IDs, amounts and payout records, but not payer details as payment records.
Provider processing does not eliminate our responsibilities for our service.

The purchase flow must identify the supplier, requested service, total price and
any HyperChat fee, creator amount, processing fee or tax, with cancellation/refund
conditions before confirmation. The applicable payment provider is identified there.
This policy does not declare all charges non-refundable merely because they are digital.
Mandatory Indian consumer and other statutory rights take precedence.

## 2. How to request help

Email **ankit@hyperchat.space** with your account/contact email, creator or service
concerned, approximate date/time, transaction reference if available, what happened
and the remedy requested. Screenshots or error text may help. Do not send passwords,
OTPs, full payment-card details, bank credentials or private OBS source URLs.

We verify requests proportionately using necessary information. Our proposed service
target is acknowledgement within 48 hours and a reasoned resolution within 30 days,
subject to shorter legal requirements. Request data follows the Privacy Policy;
a refund request does not justify unnecessary identity-document collection.

## 3. Refund eligibility

We investigate duplicate or incorrect charges, unauthorized transactions, verified
technical failures, undelivered purchased services and other cases required by law.
An appropriate remedy may be correction, agreed re-performance, a partial refund
or a full refund according to the facts and applicable rights. Report unauthorized
charges to the payment provider promptly as well.

Submitting an interaction, opening a feature or beginning a session does not by
itself prove full delivery or defeat a valid refund claim. Where a purchased
interaction is rejected, expires or cannot be delivered, we assess whether the
promised service was supplied and refund any amount legally due. Service fees
cannot be retained for an unsupplied service merely by labeling them non-refundable.
The actual charged-service and moderation rules must be disclosed before payment.

## 4. Completed interactions and creator responses

A correctly described and fully delivered digital service does not ordinarily
create a refund entitlement solely because a viewer changes their mind or dislikes
a creator's response, subject to mandatory rights. We do not guarantee audience
engagement, attention or creator endorsement. These limitations do not excuse
misrepresentation, deficient service or unlawful charges.

Queue delays and moderation can affect appearance time. If there is a material
delivery failure, contact us for investigation rather than assume submission equals delivery.

## 5. Cancellation

Contact support promptly to cancel an interaction that has not yet been delivered.
We check whether delivery can still be stopped and what remedy is due. Already
broadcast content may not be retrievable, but valid refund and privacy rights remain.

If a recurring service is offered, its renewal amount, interval, cancellation method
and effective cancellation timing must be disclosed before purchase. Cancellation
stops future renewal according to those terms and applicable law; treatment of the
current period depends on what was supplied and the applicable rights. No recurring
charge is authorized by this policy alone.

Account closure, paid-service cancellation and withdrawal of data consent must be
handled distinctly. We do not treat consent withdrawal as surrender of a lawful
refund or permission for an unwanted future charge.

## 6. Approved refunds

We communicate the approved amount and relevant payment channel. Refunds are made
to the original payment method where feasible and required. Our proposed processing
estimate is **7–14 business days after approval**, subject to applicable mandatory
deadlines. Payment-provider and bank crediting can affect when the refund appears;
we give available references and investigate delays rather than guarantee an instant credit.
The estimate must be operationally confirmed before this draft takes effect.

We retain only the transaction and accounting records needed for the applicable
period, separately from short-lived interaction/media content. See the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Creator adjustments and complaints

Refunds, chargebacks and documented lawful holds may require proportionate settlement
adjustments under the creator agreement. Streamheart provides the reason and review
route where permitted. Switching platforms alone is not a reason to confiscate
or withhold earned creator funds.

Requests may be refused for a lawful, evidenced reason, with an explanation and
review route. Repeated requests alone do not prove fraud. Our internal decision is
not unchallengeably final: payment-provider disputes, consumer remedies, competent
courts and regulators remain available under applicable law. Contact
**ankit@hyperchat.space** to request review or raise a grievance.
