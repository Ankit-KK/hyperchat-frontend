## Commencement — Rule 1, printed page 24

- Rules 1, 2, 17–21: on publication in the Official Gazette.
- Rule 4: one year after publication (Consent Manager registration/obligations).
- Rules 3, 5–16, 22, 23: eighteen months after publication.

Do not treat the notification date as independently proved publication date, or
infer the commencement of every Act provision from these Rules. Obtain the
publication record, separate Act commencement notifications, and subsequent
amendments/corrigenda before choosing exact compliance dates. This packet is a
readiness draft; it does not state all operative obligations apply on the review date.

## Changes required and made

| Provision / printed pages | Requirement in supplied PDF | Draft consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Rule 3, 24–25 | Standalone understandable notice; itemized data and specified purposes/services; particular link and methods for withdrawal, rights, Board complaints. | General policy supplemented by collection-point notices; withdrawal/rights email and support link specified. Official Board channel still to confirm before publication. |
| Rule 6, 26 | Security measures, access controls, access visibility/logs/review, continuity measures, specified logs AND personal data retained one year unless law otherwise requires, processor contract safeguards. | Security and retention plan must cover more than logs alone; no policy-only declaration of operational compliance. |
| Rule 7, 26 | Affected individuals notified without delay with five specified categories; Board initially without delay, detailed update within 72 hours or written-request extension granted by Board. | Explicit two-stage notification, contents and independent deadlines. No “wait 72 hours” initial-notice rule. |
| Rule 8(3), 27; Seventh Schedule, 40–41 | In respect of any processing by/on behalf of fiduciary, personal data, associated traffic data and other logs minimum one year from processing for Seventh Schedule purposes; then erasure unless required longer by other law/Government notification. Illustrations cover e-book transaction data even after account deletion and cloud processors. | Retract unconditional 30/90-day erasure promises. Use short active-availability limits plus restricted required archives. Do NOT read this as transaction-IDs-only or a rule applying only to the Third Schedule's large entities. Exact record scope/clock must be legally reviewed. |
| Rule 8(1)–(2), 27; Third Schedule, 35–36 | Specified entity classes/purposes: three-year inactivity period, account/token exceptions, at least 48 hours' advance notice. Thresholds: e-commerce >=2 crore registered Indian users; gaming >=50 lakh; social media >=2 crore. | Assess actual classification/count; no blanket three-year right to retain all users' data. Keep separate from general Rule 8(3). |
| Rule 9, 27 | Prominent business contact for DPO if applicable, otherwise person answering processing questions; include in rights responses. | Publish confirmed responsible person's business contact and include it in replies. Do not assume DPO/SDF status. |
| Rules 10–12, 27–29; Fourth Schedule, 36–38 | Verifiable parental/guardian consent where applicable; conditional exemptions with defined scope. | 18+ policy retained; viewer age controls required. No general streaming-platform exemption assumed. No claim that Didit completion alone proves every user's age. |
| Rule 13, 29 | If designated SDF: annual DPIA/audit, Board findings, algorithm due diligence, notified localization restrictions. | Conditional readiness item, not an assertion HyperChat is designated. |
| Rule 14, 29–30 | Publish request means and identifying particulars; reasonable grievance-response period not exceeding 90 days; nomination of one or more people. | Explain identifiers and nomination; retain shorter 30-day proposed grievance target, show statutory cap separately. |
| Rule 15, 30 | Cross-border transfers subject to specified Government requirements for availability to foreign States/controlled entities. | No blanket “all transfers permitted” or fabricated India-only guarantee; monitor actual orders and other applicable law. |
| Rule 23 and Seventh Schedule, 32, 40–41 | Authorized information demands and possible confidentiality directions. | Validate authority and scope; do not promise disclosure to users when prohibited. |
